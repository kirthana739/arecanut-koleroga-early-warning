# Koleroga Research Notes

## What is Koleroga?
Koleroga (fruit rot or mahali) is a fungal-like disease of arecanut caused by
Phytophthora species. It causes nuts to rot and fall during the monsoon,
leading to heavy yield loss.

## Conditions that favor the disease
- Continuous rain over several days
- Very high humidity (above about 90%)
- Long leaf and bunch wetness hours
- Cool, cloudy weather (roughly 20-26 C)
- Season: mainly June to September

## What the system will measure
| Factor | Sensor |
|---|---|
| Air humidity | DHT22 |
| Temperature | DHT22 |
| Rain | Rain sensor |
| Leaf wetness | Rain sensor plate used as a wetness probe |
